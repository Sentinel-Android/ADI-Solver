Privacy

How this calculator handles data.

Calculations

The calculator runs in your browser. When you submit a calculation, its inputs and results are saved in this browser’s site storage with the page’s calculation history. You can remove those entries using Clear history on the calculator or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

The hosting provider may process connection information as part of serving the website. Using a Copy button places the selected output on your device’s clipboard.

Advertising

This build does not load advertising or analytics scripts.
